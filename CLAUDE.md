## 5.2 Units and frames

mm, degrees, seconds, grams. No other units in code.
MAT frame: origin bottom-left of mat, `+X` right, `+Y` up, heading `0° = +X`, CCW positive.
Robot pose `(x, y, θ)` at the drive-axle midpoint. Object pose = base centroid + `upright: bool`.

Instances are distinguished wherever scoring needs them: bonus awards 10 per speaker to a

and the mic in the truck. S1 p2 warns of markings unused at local/national events and
S4 §8.4 describes the 8 Oct 2026 Extra Day Challenge relocating objects on the same mat.
**Do not assign them object meanings without a source.**

## 5.4 Ambiguity register

Full register with reasoning: `docs/AMBIGUITIES.md`.

| ID | Ambiguity | Conservative default |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | "moved" is *no longer touching initial position* AND *no longer upright*, but the S1 p13 photos award 0 to a clef that fell over while still in place | **OPEN** — `NEEDS-VERIFY(S6)`; implement as **OR**; flag `moved_semantics: "or" \| "and"` |
| A2 | "upright" has no angular definition | **RESOLVED** by S6 2026-06-30 — the operative test is *base fully in contact with the mat*, a **contact** predicate. `upright_tolerance_deg` demoted to a parameter |
| A3 | Does the robot overlapping a target area at time-out break "completely in"? | **RESOLVED BY IMPLICATION** (S6 2026-06-30 scored a mic as "completely inside" while the gripper held it). Default retained; evidence is an implication, not a statement |
| A4 | Must the robot return to start to stop the clock? Where does time sit in tie-break? | **RESOLVED** — S4 §10.7 has no return requirement; §10.13 ranks score first, then time |
| A5 | Do objects still held by the mechanism at time-out score? | **RESOLVED** by S6 2026-06-30 — they score at the **partial tier**, not 0. The old "no" default understated score |
| A6 | Max motors/sensors, start-size envelope, EV3+SPIKE mixing | **RESOLVED at international scope** — S4 §5.1, §5.2.x. `NEEDS-VERIFY(NO-TH)` at national scope |
| A7 | "completely in" says *no other area on the mat*, but "area" is undefined and the mat has 580 fills | **OPEN** — full containment in the target polygon is a **forced** reading, not a chosen one: any literal reading makes the game unscoreable. See ADR-013 |
| A8 | Bonus-only run: actual elapsed time, or forced 120 s under S4 §10.12? | **OPEN** — `NEEDS-VERIFY(S6)`; default: forced 120 s |

All open ambiguities now route to **S6**, not S4. S6 answers questions that were *asked* —
submitting them is an action, not a wait.

## 5.6 Scoring model (frozen v1 — machine-readable in `data/scoring_model.json`)

Max 255. Cables 2×15=30 (partial 5). Microphone 20 (partial 10). Instruments 3×15=45.
Notes 6×20=120 (partial 10). Bonus: clef 10, speakers 2×10=20, amp 10.
`completely in` = object touches the target area **and no other area on the mat** (S1 p9).
Notes are 120/255 = 47% of total.

**Partial credit is NOT uniform** — the cable is the exception:

| mission | full | partial | ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| cable | 15 | **5** | **33.3 %** |
| microphone | 20 | 10 | 50 % |
| note | 20 | 10 | 50 % |
| instrument | 15 | — | none |
| bonus | 10 | — | none |

A sloppy cable placement is worth 5; a sloppy note 10. Any EV comparison between "many
rough placements" and "few precise ones" that assumed a uniform 50 % is invalid.

**Damaged ⇒ 0, globally** (S4 §7.7) — a predicate above every mission block, not inside the
bonus block. A correctly-placed but damaged note scores 0, not 20. S1's scoring sheet never
says this.

**The 40 bonus points are a FLOOR, not a prize** (S6 2026-06-17): a run that starts and
immediately stops scores 40/255. Bonus points can only be *lost*. Every mission's EV is

    E[Δscore] = P(success) × points − P(collision) × 40

never a gross point gain.

Randomization: notes `black, white, yellow, blue` are randomly assigned to the 4 light-green
squares on the upper edge ⇒ **4! = 24 permutations**. `green` and `red` are fixed.
S4 §9.6 randomizes **after** quarantine and §10.2 forbids entering data by moving robot
parts ⇒ **the 24 permutations can only be handled by runtime sensing.**

## 5.7 Anti-patterns

1. Citing general-rules facts from memory (S4 is absent)
2. Stating any coordinate or dimension not measured from S2
3. Claiming one strategy beats another without simulator evidence
4. Hard-coding coordinates anywhere other than `field_spec.json`
5. Optimizing for 255/255 without reporting `P(success)`
6. Prose paragraphs where a table or diagram fits
7. Assuming partial credit is a uniform 50% — the cable is 5/15 = 33.3% (see §5.6)
